OViu Support

Answers to the questions we get most, and how to reach us.

Getting started

How do I add my playlist?

On the sign-in screen tap Add Playlist. Choose Xtream Codes and enter the server address, username and password your provider gave you (for example http://example.com:8080), or choose M3U URL and paste a playlist link. Then tap Save & Connect.

OViu ships with no channels of its own — it plays only what your own account gives you access to.

Can I try the app without a provider account?

Yes. Tap Try Demo Channels on the sign-in screen. It loads a small set of freely available public broadcaster channels and public domain films so you can see how the app works. No account, no sign-up.

Playback

Video keeps stalling or buffering

This is nearly always the provider's server sending data more slowly than the video needs — not the app. To check: open the player's settings (the sliders icon) and turn on Playback Stats. The overlay compares what is arriving with what the stream needs.

  • ↓ and "server too slow" — the data isn't arriving fast enough. Pausing for a minute builds a cushion, and downloading the title first always plays smoothly.
  • ↑ with a growing buffer — playback is healthy.
  • "lost" climbing quickly — the device is struggling to decode; try a lower-quality stream if your provider offers one.
A channel or film won't play at all

Check whether other titles play. If nothing plays, your provider may be blocking your connection, or the account may have reached its device limit. Some providers also restrict access by country, so a different network can give a different result.

Can I choose the audio and subtitle language once?

Yes. In the player's settings, set Audio Language and Subtitle Language; OViu applies your choice to everything you play. "Original" picks the file's first audio track, which is the most reliable option because many providers label their audio tracks incorrectly.

Downloads

How do downloads work?

Tap Download on any film or episode. Downloads continue while the app is in the background, and downloaded titles play with no internet connection — they're reachable from the sign-in screen without signing in.

Why did my download restart instead of resuming?

Resuming requires the provider's server to support partial downloads. Many IPTV servers don't, in which case the download has to start again. When OViu detects this, the paused download is labelled "restarts on resume" so it isn't a surprise.

Subscription

What does the OViu subscription include?

It unlocks the OViu app. It does not include any TV service, channels, or content — you still need your own provider account.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone or iPad open Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Mac open the App Store and click your account. Cancelling there stops any future renewal.

I paid but the app is still locked

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the app, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we'll help.
